What is kept

Your email, for signing in with a 6-digit emailed code (there is no password), held by Supabase (the service that runs the database).

What you put in: weigh-ins, blood pressure readings, meal plans and shopping checks, workouts and sets, the settings for your forecast, your blog notes, and your My Code if you save one. Each row carries your account's id, and the database only lets that account read or change it. Nobody else who uses the app can see your rows.

Your daily check-ins, other training, likes and dislikes, what's in your kitchen, recipes you add, and your conversations with the coach, the kitchen helper and the My Code builder are kept the same way: yours alone.

The AI. When you talk to the coach or the helpers, a summary of your own numbers goes to the AI provider whose key is in use, to write the reply. If you signed up yourself, that is your own key: you paste it on the AI setup page, it is stored in the app's database where no browser can read it (yours included), it is never shown again, and it is used only for you. People invited by the app's owner use the owner's key instead. Each provider keeps what it is sent under its own terms; on Gemini's free tier, Google may use it to improve its products.

The food library and recipes are the same for everyone and ship with the site.

Files you import (Gym ACE, the old app) are read in your browser. Only the rows taken from them are saved.

Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads. There are no trackers. The pages load two libraries from public CDNs (Chart.js, supabase-js) and the fonts from Google Fonts. Anyone can create an account.

On the Account page you can download everything as one file, or delete your account, which removes the sign-in and every row for good.

This is a personal tool, not medical advice. Changes to blood pressure or blood sugar medicine go through your doctor.
