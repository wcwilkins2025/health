Steadfast Health

Meal plans, weigh-ins, blood pressure and workouts to reach your goal weight. Free, no ads.

  • A week of meals for your diet: low-carb, keto, carnivore, whole-food or Mediterranean, with the shopping list built for you.
  • Weigh-ins and a forecast that shows the date you reach your goal at the pace you're keeping.
  • Blood pressure with 7- and 30-day averages.
  • A workout log with a rest timer and an exercise library, plus walks, rides, classes and sports.
  • A coach, a kitchen helper and a My Code builder that work from your own numbers, using an AI key of your own (Groq and Gemini can be tried for free).

Your numbers are yours: each account sees only its own data, and you can download or delete it any time. What is kept. A personal tool, not medical advice; blood pressure and blood sugar medicine changes go through your doctor.
