Forecast assumptions

Burn is the Mifflin-St Jeor resting rate at the current weight, times the day-to-day factor, plus the lifting, walking and riding set on Training. It falls as you get lighter, so the line bends. 3,500 kcal is one pound. Models run 10 to 20 percent off; the scale wins.
